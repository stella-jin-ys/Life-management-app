# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite, delegated to Codex and approved in the implementation specification.

## Users

People who want one private-feeling place to notice small daily progress, name difficult emotions without shame, and maintain gentle momentum across mental and physical wellbeing.

## Product Purpose

Life Management helps users track their mental, psychological, and physical state while deliberately returning attention to positive moments, emotional comfort, health habits, and achievable next steps.

## Positioning

The product combines positive evidence from daily life with non-judgmental emotional validation: it celebrates small wins while making low-energy states feel common and survivable.

## Operating Context

The first experience is a daily home dashboard used for a brief mood check-in, recording a highlight, checking health routines, receiving comfort, and reviewing the next goal milestone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core areas are Dashboard, Highlights, Low Battery, Diet & Health, and Goals.
- A desktop side menu keeps core areas visible and previews upcoming features.
- The side menu also previews Tasks, Finance, Study, Workout, Sleeping, Diary, and Settings.
- The first version is a local prototype with authored demo data and local UI state.
- Shared-feeling percentages are demo community signals, not population research.
- Compliments are deterministic local responses, not a claim of live AI infrastructure.
- The product is supportive wellbeing software, not clinical or medical guidance.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Life Management.
- Voice: warm, affirming, calm, direct, and never guilt-inducing.
- Progress language emphasizes noticing, continuing, and small steps.

## Evidence on Hand

No external research dataset, testimonials, clinical evidence, brand assets, or production user data are available. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make small progress visible and worth celebrating.
- Validate difficult feelings without turning them into identity or diagnosis.
- Prefer gentle next steps over pressure, streaks, and gamified urgency.
- Keep emotional and physical wellbeing understandable at a glance.
- Label demo or synthetic signals honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core interactions must be keyboard accessible, use readable contrast, and communicate state with text or icons in addition to color.
